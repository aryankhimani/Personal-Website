## What I did

- Co-led an 8-month project to run the conference: 400+ attendees and 13 student talks. I coordinated a 50-person volunteer team, the board, sponsors and logistics.
- Designed and ran 10+ activation stations with partners including Science World, UN Girl Up and Genome BC.
- Gave a 10-minute talk the year before, "Did Curiosity Actually Kill the Cat?", at the Ismaili Centre Vancouver. It's about curiosity and risk-taking, with a little Tom and Jerry. I helped with fundraising, merchandise and activities too.

## Why

I went through TED's public-speaking curriculum in a TED-Ed club. Giving a talk had been a goal of mine for a long time.
