## What it is

Through a TED-Ed club I worked through TED's public-speaking curriculum, and in 2024 I gave a talk in Vancouver. Giving one had been a goal of mine for a long time.

## What I did

- Gave a 10-minute talk, "Did Curiosity Actually Kill the Cat?", at the Ismaili Centre Vancouver. It's about curiosity, risk-taking and discovery at any age, with humour and a little Tom and Jerry.
- Helped organize that event too: fundraising, merchandise and activities.
- Then co-led the 8-month project to run the next conference: 400+ attendees and 13 student talks, coordinating a 50-person volunteer team, the board, sponsors and logistics.
- Designed and ran 10+ activation stations with partners including Science World, UN Girl Up and Genome BC.
