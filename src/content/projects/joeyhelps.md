## What it is

JoeyHelps matched Gen Z users with licensed mental health providers through a short quiz. We built it in the Junior Achievement Company Program, backed by Deloitte and JA.

## What I did

- Owned product development: wireframes, 15+ Figma pages and mobile prototypes.
- Ran A/B tests and live user testing to improve the matching flow.
- Hosted the First Steps Summit at Deloitte, turning 1,000+ event page views into 200+ sign-ups and 70+ attendees.
- Pitched to Google, Walmart and CPP Investments.

## What came of it

- $2,645 in revenue, with 100+ users completing the matching quiz.
- JoeyHelps won the Ingenious+ Award and $1,000 in an external JA competition.
- I won the JA Extra Mile Award, with a $1,000 scholarship.
