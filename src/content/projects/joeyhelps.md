## What I built

- The product: wireframes, 15+ Figma pages and mobile prototypes.
- A better matching flow, through A/B tests and live user testing.
- The First Steps Summit at Deloitte. 1,000+ page views turned into 200+ sign-ups and 70+ attendees.

## What came of it

- $2,645 in revenue, with 100+ people completing the matching quiz.
- JoeyHelps won the Ingenious+ Award and $1,000 in an external JA competition.
- I won the JA Extra Mile Award, with a $1,000 scholarship.
- We pitched to Google, Walmart and CPP Investments.

## What it is

JoeyHelps matched Gen Z with licensed mental health providers. We built it in the Junior Achievement Company Program, backed by Deloitte and JA.
