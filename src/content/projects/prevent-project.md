## What it is

PREVENT, with Dr. Karim Keshavjee, works on diabetes prevention. Our team built a gamified electronic medical record (EMR) app for people with pre-diabetes.

## What I did

- Led the tech team, designed the interface and coded the frontend.
- Designed the engagement features: goals, badges, leaderboards and rewards.
- Brought Figma to the team, so UI design and prototyping happened in one shared tool.
