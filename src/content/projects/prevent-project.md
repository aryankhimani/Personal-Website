## What I built

- The frontend of the app. I designed the interface, then coded it.
- The parts that keep people coming back: goals, badges and leaderboards.

## My role

- I led the tech team, working with clinical experts on personalized ways to help patients.
- I brought Figma to the team, so design and prototyping happened in one shared tool.

## What it is

PREVENT, with Dr. Karim Keshavjee, works on diabetes prevention. Our part was an electronic medical record (EMR) app that feels a bit like a game.
