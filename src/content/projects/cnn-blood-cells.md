## What it is

The Canadian Patient Safety Institute estimated that up to 28,000 deaths a year in Canada are caused by medical errors. I wanted to see whether a CNN could do a careful medical task as well as a person.

## What I did

- Trained a model on images of four kinds of white blood cells: neutrophils, eosinophils, monocytes and lymphocytes.
- Wrote up how CNNs work in a Medium article.
