## What I built

- An image classifier for four kinds of white blood cells: neutrophils, eosinophils, monocytes and lymphocytes.
- A Medium article that explains how CNNs work.

## Why

The Canadian Patient Safety Institute estimated that up to 28,000 deaths a year in Canada come from medical errors. I wanted to see whether a CNN could do a careful medical task as well as a person.
