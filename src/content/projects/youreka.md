## What it is

Youreka Canada runs university-level research programs for high school students. Our team looked at how traumatic experiences connect to the way genes are expressed.

## What I did

- Analysed gene expression data in R to find genes expressed differently in people with a history of PTSD.
- Turned the work into a research paper, "Genetics in Trauma Response: Identifying Differentially Expressed Genes Linked to PTSD History", guided by mentors from the University of Toronto.

## What came of it

- Finalist at the Youreka Canada National Qualifiers (May 2023).
- A poster at the 2023 Youreka National Symposium.
