## What it is

Clubs were where I tried big ideas first. I was in the MaST program (math, science and technology) at Danforth Collegiate and Technical Institute.

## What I did

- President of Model UN and the Biology Club, and founder of InnovateX, a club for real-world problem solving.
- Grew Model UN from 10 to 80+ members, raised $2,000 for conferences and took delegates to them.
- Took 20 Biology Club members to HOSA competitions.

## What came of it

- Best Delegate at Toronto Model UN (2023) and at OxfordMUN Canada (2024).
- Top 10 in HOSA public speaking (2023).
