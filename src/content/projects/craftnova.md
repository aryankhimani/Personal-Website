## What it is

I co-founded CraftNova because AI was making the job market feel uncertain, and a lot of people my age didn't know where to start. CraftNova turns career research into clear guidance anyone can read for free, and it breaks stereotypes about blue-collar work.

## What I did

- Led product development end to end, starting from what students actually needed.
- Created the sustainable "Nova Kits", which spotlight opportunities in the skilled trades.
