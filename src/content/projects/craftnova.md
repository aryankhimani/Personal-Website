## What I built

- The product, end to end, starting from what students actually needed.
- The sustainable "Nova Kits", which show off opportunities in the skilled trades.

## Why

AI was making the job market feel uncertain, and a lot of people my age didn't know where to start. We turned career research into clear guidance anyone can read for free. CraftNova came out of the JA Company Program, backed by Deloitte.
