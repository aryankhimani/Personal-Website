## What it is

The New York Academy of Sciences' Junior Academy takes about 5% of applicants worldwide. Members work in global teams on real-world problems.

## What I did

- Worked with teammates around the world on Eudaimonia, an app for "cognitive classrooms". It has mood check-ins and stress-relief tips, and is designed to add AI that reads emotional cues like facial expression and tone.
- The first version is a Swift MVP. The code and the pitch deck are linked below.
